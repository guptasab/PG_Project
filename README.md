# ⚡ RIVALS 3D - First Person Shooter

A fast-paced, high-octane 3D First Person Shooter inspired by the Roblox hit game **Rivals**. Built entirely in vanilla WebGL with Three.js and the Web Audio API, featuring zero external runtime dependencies.

---

## ⚡ Easy Mode & Non-Targeting Bots (10x Easier!)

- **Bots Do Not Target You**: Bots are now peaceful practice targets that roam between waypoints or fire harmlessly into the distance. They will never chase, hunt, or shoot at you.
- **Bot Damage is 0**: You cannot be eliminated by bots.
- **Super Player Vitals**: 500 Health + 500 Shield with almost instantaneous continuous shield regeneration.
- **1-2 Shot Gun Power**:
  - Assault Rifle: 45 body / 90 head (1-2 shot kill)
  - Pump Shotgun: 224 damage burst (instant kill)
  - Viper Sniper: 250 body / 500 head (instant one-shot obliteration)
  - Dual SMGs: 32 body / 64 head (80 round mag)
  - Revolver: 110 body / 220 head (instant kill)
  - Melee Knife / Katana: 150 - 250 damage (one-hit kill)
- **Massive Magazine Capacities & Fast Reload**: Double ammo capacity on all guns with 0.6s - 0.8s reload time.
- **Pinpoint Laser Accuracy**: Recoil reduced by 90% and bullet spread eliminated.
- **10x Reward Rain**:
  - Eliminations award **+50 Gems** (was 10), and Headshots award **+100 Gems** (was 15)!
  - Quests require fewer kills (1-2 kills) and award **15 - 30 Keys** each!
  - 25 Starter Keys & 500 Starter Gems so you can buy weapons and open crates right away.

## 🌟 Core Features & Systems

### 1. 🎯 Dynamic First Person Combat & Movement
- **Pointer Lock Controls**: Smooth 360° mouse aiming with pitch clamping.
- **Sprint & Dynamic FOV**: Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to sprint with speed boost and camera FOV expansion.
- **Slide Dashing**: Press <kbd>C</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> while sprinting to initiate a slide dash with camera tilt and slide friction sound.
- **Jump Pads**: Interactive arena pads that launch you high into the air for aerial frags.
- **Aim Down Sights (ADS)**: Right-click to zoom in, tighten bullet spread, and aim down sights.
- **Hitmarkers & Floating Damage Numbers**:
  - White hitmarkers & damage text for body hits.
  - Golden critical hitmarkers, high-pitch "DING!" audio chime, and `CRIT! 150` text for headshots.
- **Tactical Health & Shield**:
  - 100 Health + 50 Armor/Shield.
  - Shield absorbs incoming damage first and automatically regenerates after 4 seconds out of combat.
