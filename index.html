💎 GEMS & 🔑 KEYS ECONOMY

💎
GEMS (FROM KILLS)

Eliminate AI bots in matches to earn 10 Gems per kill. Land a headshot for a +5 Gem bonus!

➔ Used in the Shop to buy Weapon Crates to unlock rare & legendary Gun Skins!
🔑
KEYS (FROM QUESTS)

Complete match challenges in the Quests Tab (e.g., getting kills, headshots, or sniper eliminations).

➔ Used in the Loadout to permanently unlock New Guns (Shotgun, Sniper, SMGs, Revolver, Katana)!

🎯 PRO TIPS FOR SURVIVAL

  • Aim for the Head: Headshots deal 2x critical damage with a gold damage number and instant ding sound!
  • Slide to Evade: Sprinting and pressing C initiates a high-velocity slide, making you harder for bots to hit.
  • Shield Regeneration: You have 100 Health + 50 Shield. If you take damage, stay behind cover for 4 seconds to regenerate your shield.
  • Jump Pads in Arena: Step onto circular glowing pads in the Neon Arena to launch into the air for aerial dominance!
